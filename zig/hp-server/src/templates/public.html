The story

A four year old budget phone, transformed into a fullstack platform you can deploy to in thirty seconds.

The hardware is unremarkable. Snapdragon 720G, 8 GB of RAM, 128 GB of storage, originally meant to play games and run social apps.

What makes it a server is a single Zig binary, a Cloudflare Tunnel that holds the wire open, and a recovery system that will not let it die.
